Guide

Getting started

Hold still on a tree to punch out logs, then open 🎒 to turn them into planks, sticks and a crafting table. Tap the table to make tools. A stone pickaxe opens up the world underground.

Staying alive

Hunger drains as you move and dig; eat to refill it. A hearty meal (6 or more food) leaves you well fed: hunger falls half as fast and you heal quicker. Night brings creatures: build a bed and sleep through it, or through a storm. Cold lands chill you (stay by a fire or drink honey tea); deserts at midday cause heatstroke (find shade or water). Spiders poison; healing tea cures poison, chill and heat.

Metal and machines

Smelt ores in a smelter, crush them in a rock crusher for double the metal. Water wheels, windmills and the steam engine drive shafts and gears that run saws, mills, drills, trip hammers and harvesters. Pumps move water and lava through lead pipes. Levers, buttons, pressure plates, observers and gauges send signals to doors, lamps and rails. Throw things (Q, or hold a hotbar slot) onto a running belt or a hopper to send them along by hand.

Automation

Copper wire carries a signal along as far as it runs. An inverter gives a signal while it gets none, a delay block passes its signal on after a moment (tap to set ½ to 5 s), and a latch flips each time its signal comes on; all three take their signal from behind and give it out in front. An inverter feeding a delay block that loops back round makes a clock. Detector rails signal while a cart is on them. Ejectors throw things out of a chest, collectors pull in loose things, splitting and sorting belts send things to the side, bucket elevators lift them, and an auto-crafter makes whatever its item frame shows. Link shafts with a drive belt, store spin in a flywheel, read a network with a tachometer and double its speed with a signalled gear shift. Tanks hold water or lava, valves open pipes on a signal, lava piped to an oven, smelter or steam engine is its fuel, and sprinklers make crops grow faster. Farm with tree fellers, planters, block breakers and placers, animal stations and fishing nets.

Exploring

Carry a map to see the land around you; press M (or ☰ → World map) for the whole of what you've explored, and tap it to set waypoints. Rivers hold fish, villages hold traders, and shrines and lairs hold guardians worth defeating. Altars let you enchant tools with crystal shards.

Building

Walls, pillars and stairs join up on their own (stairs make corners). Paint doors and glass with dye, hang paintings, and light rooms with lanterns, chandeliers or coloured glow lamps.

Creative mode

Choose it for a new world, or switch under ☰ → Game mode. You can't be hurt or get hungry, blocks break at once and are never used up, and the inventory has a palette of every block and item. Press F (or 🕊) to fly: jump rises, Shift (or ⤓) sinks. The copy wand copies builds: tap one corner, then the opposite corner, then tap wherever you want copies; double-tap and hold (or hold the mouse button) to start over.
