📖 Guidebook

Guide

Getting started

Hold still on a tree to punch out logs, then open 🎒 to turn them into planks, sticks and a crafting table. Tap the table to make tools. A stone pickaxe opens up the world underground.

Staying alive

Hunger drains as you move and dig; eat to refill it. A hearty meal (6 or more food) leaves you well fed: hunger falls half as fast and you heal quicker. Night brings creatures: build a bed and sleep through it, or through a storm. Cold lands chill you (stay by a fire or drink honey tea); deserts at midday cause heatstroke (find shade or water). Spiders poison; healing tea cures poison, chill and heat.

Metal and machines

Smelt ores in a smelter, crush them in a rock crusher for double the metal. Water wheels, windmills and the steam engine drive shafts and gears that run saws, mills, drills, trip hammers and harvesters. Pumps move water and lava through lead pipes. Levers, buttons, pressure plates, observers and gauges send signals to doors, lamps and rails. Throw things (Q, or hold a hotbar slot) onto a running belt or a hopper to send them along by hand.

Automation

Copper wire carries a signal along as far as it runs. An inverter gives a signal while it gets none, a delay block passes its signal on after a moment (tap to set ½ to 5 s), and a latch flips each time its signal comes on; all three take their signal from behind and give it out in front. An inverter feeding a delay block that loops back round makes a clock. Detector rails signal while a cart is on them. Ejectors throw things out of a chest, collectors pull in loose things, splitting and sorting belts send things to the side, bucket elevators lift them, and an auto-crafter makes whatever its item frame shows. Link shafts with a drive belt, store spin in a flywheel, read a network with a tachometer and double its speed with a signalled gear shift. Tanks hold water or lava, valves open pipes on a signal, lava piped to an oven, smelter or steam engine is its fuel, and sprinklers make crops grow faster. Farm with tree fellers, planters, block breakers and placers, animal stations and fishing nets.

Electricity

A generator makes electricity while a shaft turns it. Electrum wire carries it to electric lamps, the electric pulverizer (a faster rock crusher that gets more dust out of ore) and the battery charger; machines set right beside each other join up too. Tap a generator, lamp or wire to see whether there's enough power. Things built round a battery keep its charge and top up from batteries you carry: the portable lamp (tap to switch on), the jackhammer (a fast pickaxe that sometimes shakes out ore dust) and the jetpack (wear it as a chestplate and hold jump in the air).

Solar panels and wind turbines make electricity without a shaft, and cupronickel cable carries it up walls and across ceilings. A grid battery stores what's spare for when you're short; a power meter signals while the grid is short. Tap an electric lamp to have it come on only with a signal (a lever, or a daylight sensor, which signals at night). An electric motor turns shafts, and the electric smelter smelts with no fuel. More battery gear: the electric drill (digs 3 × 3), hover boots, night-vision goggles and the magnet ring (tap to switch on).

The programmable crafter steps through its item frames on a signal; an item counter pulses after every few things pass through; teleport pads pair up in the order you put them down and send things (or you, standing on a glowing one) to each other; and the quarry digs out a 9 × 9 area down to bedrock. Dungeons hold monster spawners: creatures die in lava, on cactus and from long falls, and leave their drops for collectors.

Power, uranium and gear

A geothermal generator makes power from lava beside it. A wireless charger tops up your battery gear within 8 blocks, electric rails speed carts along, and a lift carries you to the next lift above or below (jump to go up, sneak to go down). Deep uranium ore makes fuel rods for a reactor: lots of power, but keep water round it or it overheats and melts down (a signal stops it). The chainsaw fells trees and gives sawdust; sawdust and water make paper, and 6 paper and a hide make a book. Backpacks carry more, the spanner turns machines (hold it to pick one up with what's in it), and the grappling hook pulls you up to a block (jump to climb onto it).

Nuclear waste and new machines

Burnt-out fuel rods become spent rods, which give off radiation: shut them in a lead-lined chest or wear a hazmat suit. A centrifuge makes enriched uranium for longer-lasting rods. Ice and packed ice from a freezer cool a reactor better than water. A tesla coil zaps night creatures, an electric fence keeps animals in and creatures out, and the arc furnace smelts fast and makes invar and starsteel. Bookshelves round an altar make enchanting cheaper and unlock Lucky, Mending and Feather Fall. A compost bin makes fertiliser for crops and saplings, and a journal holds your notes.

Getting about

Row a boat on water; fly a hot-air balloon (jump to rise, sneak to sink; it burns coal); run zipline rope between two posts and slide along it with a grappling hook; and stop carts at rail stations until a signal sends them on. Saddle a buffalo to ride it. Hold a cable car and tap a zipline post to ride the line in a gondola, uphill too.

Breeding

Feed two animals of a kind their food (seeds for ducks and chickens, wheat for buffalo, alpacas, yaks and camels, berries for newts, berries or wheat for pigs, berries for deer, fish for tame wolves and cats) and they'll have a young one, with the coat of one of its parents. An animal station with their food in it does this for you. Chickens lay eggs and pigs dig up truffles. Throw a stick and a tame wolf fetches it; a tame cat sometimes leaves you a present in the morning. Creep up on deer holding sneak, or they bolt.

Exploring

Carry a map to see the land around you; press M (or ☰ → World map) for the whole of what you've explored, and tap it to set waypoints. Rivers hold fish, villages hold traders, and shrines and lairs hold guardians worth defeating. Look out for volcanoes, old ruins and shipwrecks with loot chests, and for meteorites falling at night: their rock gives meteoric iron for starsteel tools. Altars let you enchant tools with crystal shards. Lighthouses stand on the coasts; carry an explorer's map to mark what you find, and read a treasure map to find where to dig.

Building

Walls, pillars and stairs join up on their own (stairs make corners). Paint doors and glass with dye, hang paintings, and light rooms with lanterns, chandeliers, coloured glow lamps or beeswax candles. Sit on chairs, show an item on a shelf or table, and plant flower pots, hanging baskets and window boxes.

Farming and food

Pick apples, cherries and oranges from fruit trees in summer and autumn. A cooking pot makes pies and soups that give buffs. Glass over crops makes a greenhouse that grows in winter, a scarecrow keeps crows off your wheat, and a farm controller signals when your field is ripe. An apiary gives honeycomb and beeswax.

Villages, weather and crops

Villagers go home at night, and the bell rings each morning. Put things on a market stall and villagers buy them for coins; trade with a village and it grows. Shelter from thunderstorms: lightning starts fires, and hail in the mountains hurts without a helmet. Grow carrots, potatoes, pumpkins and grapes (on a trellis), and change crops now and then: the same crop again and again tires the soil. Press grapes and curds, then leave them in an oak barrel to become wine and cheese.

Temples, islands and trains

Sunken temples and sky islands hide vaults that open when you solve their levers or plates. Dive with a diving helmet, flippers and an air pump. Radios carry a signal between any two on the same channel (tap to change). A sorting machine sends things down the tube framed for them. A locomotive burns coal and pulls the carts behind it from station to station. A fireplace warms a room: give it a chimney.

Horses, villages and festivals

Feed a wild horse apples, carrots or sugar until it trusts you, then saddle it and ride; each has its own speed and jump. Tap a stable to bring your horses home. Tap a village bell to hear what it wants today, and again holding it to deliver: requests, trades and stall sales raise your reputation, and friends of a village pay less. On the fourth day of each season villages hold a festival, with double pay and fireworks at night.

Kitchen garden, kiln and cellar

Grow onions, beans (which enrich the soil), tomatoes and herbs for stews, soups and baked beans. Press apples and berries, then age the juice in a barrel into cider and berry wine: a dark cellar ages it faster. A kiln fires clay into terracotta, glazed tiles and urns.

Ropes, locks and caves

A rope ladder unrolls down a wall or cliff edge; a rope bridge lays itself across a gap. Build a canal lock: a walled chamber with lock gates and a sluice wheel that fills or drains it to the next canal's level. Beware falling stalactites in dark caverns; tap glowing cave beetles for shells to make lamps.

Ore washing and machines

Crushed ore comes out as dirty dust. Smelt it as it is, or wash it in an ore washer (it needs power and water touching it) for clean dust and a chance of a trace of another metal. A sieve sifts gravel and dirt by hand. Tap a machine with a speed, efficiency or yield upgrade to fit it. To make charcoal, cover a stack of logs with earth and light one log with a torch.

Horses, honey, fishing and boats

Saddlebags and horse armour go on your horse. Fill a stable's manger with wheat or hay and your horses come out rested. Bees make honey after the flowers near them. Carry worms when you fish, or bait a fish trap with them. Fine crops win prizes at festival shows. A chest boat carries a chest, and a storage cart behind a locomotive keeps it in coal.

Guidebook, music and logistics pipes

Press G or tap 📖 for the guidebook: goals to reach, with rewards to claim. Set the music's volume in the menu. Logistics pipes make a network: a provider pipe on a chest offers what's in it, a crafting pipe on an auto-crafter or machine lets the network make one thing, a supplier pipe keeps a chest stocked, a sink pipe takes things in, and an extractor pipe empties a chest into the sinks. Tap a pipe to set it up. Put a power junction on the network, wired to the grid, and a request table to order from: it works out any crafting needed. A remote orderer, linked to a table, lets you order from afar. A stock sensor pipe signals while the network runs short of something; a power junction getting a signal pauses its network. Liquid provider and supplier pipes move water and lava between tanks, and feed lava to ovens, smelters and engines. A liquid sink pipe takes in liquid with nowhere else to go, and a bottler fills buckets from it. Drone pads on the same channel join two networks, and courier drones fly between them; a network with a drone pad stays loaded however far away you go.

Backpack tips

Pin a recipe (📌) to keep what it needs on screen. When the stack in your hand runs out, the next one moves in from your backpack. Double-tap a slot to gather a stack together; shift-click (or press and hold) to move a stack across to a chest or between hotbar and backpack. Tips show what each thing is used in, and a compass points to your nearest waypoint. Type in Find in chests nearby to see which chests hold something, and where. Photo mode (☰ or F2) hides the HUD and lets the camera float free for a picture.

Creative mode

Choose it for a new world, or switch under ☰ → Game mode. You can't be hurt or get hungry, blocks break at once and are never used up, and the inventory has a palette of every block and item. Press F (or 🕊) to fly: jump rises, Shift (or ⤓) sinks. The copy wand copies builds: tap one corner, then the opposite corner, then tap wherever you want copies; double-tap and hold (or hold the mouse button) to start over.
